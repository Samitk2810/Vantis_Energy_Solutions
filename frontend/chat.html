Cost, financing and subsidies

Good solar planning starts with the project details—not a one-size-fits-all quote.

Ask how cost is decided ↗

No single price list

Project cost depends on system size, equipment, installation requirements, property, location and overall project complexity. A consultation helps scope your project.

Financing

Options may be available depending on the project, financial institution and your eligibility. Approval and monthly payments can't be promised upfront.

Government subsidies

Program rules, eligibility and amounts can change. Current benefits need to be verified for your situation; the team can explain what to consider.

Good to know

Common questions

Need a more specific answer? Ask the Vantis assistant.

Ask a question ↗
What does Vantis Energy Solutions do?

Vantis helps homes and small businesses explore rooftop solar, including requirements, system options, installation and ongoing support.

Do you install solar panels?

Vantis designs and installs rooftop solar in selected cities. Share your city so the team can check current availability.

Can you help me choose a solar system?

Yes. The reference systems are a starting point, not a final recommendation. A suitable configuration depends on electricity use, rooftop area and a site assessment.

How much does solar installation cost?

Cost varies with system size, equipment, installation requirements, property, location and project complexity. Vantis doesn't publish one fixed price for every project.

How much can I save?

Savings depend on your usage, system design, site conditions, utility rules and other factors. Vantis can't promise a specific saving before your project is assessed.

Do you provide financing?

Financing options may be available depending on the project, provider and eligibility. Approval and payment terms need to be confirmed with the provider.

Can you help with subsidies?

Subsidy schemes and eligibility change. The team can help you understand what to verify, but current benefits should be confirmed for your specific project.

When will my system be installed?

Timing depends on project requirements, site conditions, equipment availability, documentation and scheduling. A date can only be discussed after assessment.

Do you provide maintenance?

Vantis offers monitoring and maintenance support. If you're an existing customer, tell the assistant what is happening so the team can follow up.

Can I get a callback?

Ask the assistant to request a callback and it can guide you through sharing the information needed for the Vantis team.
